# Research methods

The project examined accessible interaction with automated-vehicle interfaces. I used different methods to address different parts of that question.

| Method | My work | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contextual inquiry | I examined users’ experiences and the context around vehicle interaction. | Frame design questions around access needs. |
| Participatory design | I facilitated activities that brought users’ perspectives into interface concepts. | Involve users in shaping the design. |
| Prototyping | I built Figma and Bubble interfaces. | Make concepts concrete enough to discuss and evaluate. |
| Recruitment and moderation | I recruited participants, communicated tasks and moderated sessions. | Support accessible participation and clear study interactions. |
| On-road evaluation | I ran study sessions that placed interface interactions within a journey context. | Examine how users understand and use controls in context. |
| Analysis and reporting | I coded qualitative material, analyzed quantitative evidence and wrote findings with the team. | Connect observations and participant explanations to design implications. |

## Breadth of the prototype work

The Figma collection includes mobile-app and in-vehicle HMI versions for deaf and hard-of-hearing participants, low-vision participants, participants with motor impairments and participants without disabilities. Additional files represent group and session versions.

The selected DHH arrival example illustrates one need within that broader work. It is not the full participant population or the entire prototype collection.

## Interpreting the evidence

Observations can reveal an interaction difficulty, interviews can explain the experience behind it, and quantitative measures can summarize patterns. A prototype makes a design decision visible; evaluation is needed to establish how well it works.

I used on-road sessions, observation and participant feedback to evaluate interface interactions. The public material presents research responsibilities, selected prototype evidence and design rationale. It does not present participant-level records or unpublished study results.
